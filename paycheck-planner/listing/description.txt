Know exactly what's safe to spend until your next payday, without missing a bill.

Paycheck to Paycheck Planner answers one question on the first screen: how much can I spend before I next get paid? It takes your balance, subtracts the bills due before payday and a small cushion, and shows what's left. No categories to fill in, no spreadsheets.

WHO IT'S FOR
• You get paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, and bills land in between
• You've tried budgets with dozens of categories and stopped using them
• You want to know "can I buy this?" before you buy it

WHAT IT DOES
• Safe to spend: one clear number until your next payday, plus about how much a day
• Can I afford this? Type a price and it checks every payday and bill for the next 90 days
• Until payday: your balance day by day for 90 days, with the tightest day flagged early
• What if: try a bigger expense or a change in spending before it happens
• Bills & pay: set rent, phone, car, subscriptions and your pay once; they repeat on their own
• Pay schedules: weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, every 4 weeks, monthly or every set number of days
• Quick log: type "coffee 4.50" and it comes off your number straight away
• Check off bills when they leave and confirm payday when it lands
• 8 color themes, including two dark ones
• Sample mode to explore with made-up data first

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No bank login, no account, no subscription
• Works offline in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on Mac and PC
• Your data is saved on your own computer and never sent anywhere
• One-click backups, or an automatic daily backup to a folder you choose (Chrome and Edge)

WHAT YOU GET
• One HTML file. Download it, double-click it, and it opens in your browser
• A welcome tour that sets up your number in about a minute

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a digital download. Nothing physical is shipped
• It's a planning tool, not financial advice, and it doesn't connect to your bank
• Personal use by one buyer. Please don't share or resell the file
