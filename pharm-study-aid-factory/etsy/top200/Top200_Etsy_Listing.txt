Study the 200 most-prescribed drugs, organized the way you actually learn them.

These printable, double-sided flashcards cover the Top 200 drugs, grouped by body system and drug family, so related drugs sit together (all the beta blockers, all the SSRIs, all the statins) and patterns are easier to remember.

• A "My Notes" box for your own memory tricks

Back:
• Drug class, color-coded by body system, for quick self-quizzing
