## Description
Know what your Etsy shop really keeps. Shop Insights turns the files Etsy already gives you into a clear, beautiful dashboard: your take-home after every fee, ad and credit, for one shop or several.

Try it free first: [link to the free take-home check]

Works on Mac and PC in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). Everything stays on your computer. No logins, no server, no subscription.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your shop (and your logo, if you like)
2. Drop in your Etsy downloads: payment account statements, sold order items, listings, reviews and deposits
3. Review your summaries: every tab fills in by itself
Importing the same file twice is safe: anything already there is skipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• Dashboard: take-home, revenue, orders, average order, Etsy fees, your Etsy Ads, net profit and cash flow, with a monthly goal
• Insights: a shop health score, a to-do list you can check off, and the products to double down on or revisit
• Your Etsy statement, laid out like Etsy's own and matching it to the cent, with payouts to your bank
• Fees & ads: every fee, ad, credit and Offsite Ads charge, per order and per month
• Year at a glance and Year over year: ten years side by side, and each shop's profit over time
• Several shops: switch between them or see them all together, with your own logos
• Products: sales, profit per product after Etsy's cut and your own costs, and listing health
• New listings: what each month's new listings went on to sell
• Buyers & reviews: repeat buyers, countries, when your top countries buy, and every review
• Profit & loss by month, quarter or year
• Tax: quarterly set-aside and a Schedule C summary for your accountant
• Pricing calculator, coupons and seasonality
• 8 color themes, light and dark

PRIVACY
Your files are read on your computer and never uploaded. Buyer names, emails and addresses in your exports are never stored: only the country and a scrambled buyer key (to count repeat buyers).

GOOD TO KNOW
• Digital download: you receive one HTML file. Nothing is shipped.
• Best on a computer or tablet.
• Download a backup now and then from Settings (or connect a backup folder).
• Screenshots show made-up sample shops.
• Shop Insights is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Etsy, Inc.
• It organizes your records; it is not tax advice.
