## Description

Stop typing transactions. Drop in your bank and card statements and see exactly where your money goes, sorted for you.

Money Autopilot is a money tracker in a single file. Download the CSV from each bank and card, drop them all in at once, and it reads every line, cleans up the merchant names, sorts each one into a category and shows your month: money in, spending, what you kept, and where it all went. No bank login, no account linking, no subscription.
