A rental property tracker for small landlords: a rent roll that shows who paid and who's late, every expense on its Schedule E line, and each property's real cash flow, for any dates you pick. It opens in any browser and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Rental Property Tracker is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add your properties and units, then your tenants with their rent, due day, lease dates and deposit. From then on the rent roll shows what's due each month, what arrived and who's late (with your late fee ready to add); expenses go on their Schedule E lines and repeat on their own; and Finances, Profit & loss and Schedule E add everything up by property, for any dates. Log repairs and tenant requests, bill a tenant for damage or utilities, print letters and notices filled in from your records, and bring in your bank's CSV when you want to. No bank login, no subscription. Your figures and your tenants' details stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the short tour, or explore the fictional sample landlord's three properties first.
3. Add a property: its name, how many units, what it's worth and the mortgage payment.
4. Add your tenants: rent, the day it's due, lease dates, deposit and late fee.
5. Each month, record rent as it arrives and add expenses (or import your bank's CSV). The rent roll, profit & loss and Schedule E fill themselves.
