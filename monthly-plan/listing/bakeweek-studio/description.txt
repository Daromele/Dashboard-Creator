A recipe cost calculator and home bakery business planner in one: cost every recipe from your own pack prices, price it for the margin you want, then plan the week. Orders, pre-orders, bread clubs and market days turn into one bake plan, one shopping list and one set of batch sheets. It opens in any browser and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Bakeweek Studio is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add your pantry with the pack sizes and prices you pay, save your tested recipes with yields and hands-on minutes, and every recipe shows its cost per piece, your price and your margin. Take custom orders, post a weekly pre-order menu, set up standing orders for regulars and cafés, and plan your market stall. The week plan adds it all up: what to buy, what to bake each day, what to pack for each pickup, and whether it fits in the hours you have. No subscription, no account. Your recipes and customers stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the short tour, or explore the fictional sample bakery first.
3. Add your ingredients with their pack sizes and prices, then your recipes with yields and times.
4. Add orders, a pre-order menu, standing orders and market days as they come.
5. Each week, follow the shopping list, batch sheets and packing list. Mark orders collected and paid.
