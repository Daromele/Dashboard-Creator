A monthly budget planner that does the adding up for you, shows the whole year at a glance, and opens in any browser without ever asking you to sign up for anything.

Monthly Plan is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page, except everything inside it is yours to edit. Set your plan once and it repeats every month, so you adjust a few numbers instead of rebuilding a spreadsheet from scratch. Log what you spend, or import your bank's CSV and let it sort the entries for you. The dashboard shows one honest number: what's left after your commitments, not just what's in the account. No bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a fictional household's year first.
3. Build your plan once: income, bills, subscriptions, debt payments, everyday spending and savings. Every new month starts from it.
4. Log what you spend as you go, or import your bank's CSV and let it sort the entries for you.
5. Check the dashboard and the two-minute weekly review: what's left after your commitments, and what stands out.
