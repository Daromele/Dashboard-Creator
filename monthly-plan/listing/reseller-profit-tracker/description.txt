A reseller spreadsheet that does the math for you: every item with what it really cost, every sale with the platform's fees and the shipping label, returns, and the true profit you actually kept, by item, by platform and by month. It opens in any browser and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Reseller Profit Tracker is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. After a haul, type each item's title and what you paid and press Enter; bought a bin or a lot for one price, and the cost is split across the items for you. When something sells, pick the platform and the price: the usual fee is filled in for you to check, and the profit appears before you save. Returns go back on the shelf. Your inventory, sales, running costs and mileage add up to a profit & loss for any dates and a Schedule C with cost of goods sold. No subscription, no bank login. Your numbers stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the short tour, or explore the fictional sample shop first.
3. Add your inventory: a title and what you paid is enough. Add the list price and where it's listed when you list it.
4. When an item sells, record the sale: platform, price, shipping. Check the fee and the label.
5. Check Home for this month's true profit. At tax time, print the profit & loss and Schedule C.
