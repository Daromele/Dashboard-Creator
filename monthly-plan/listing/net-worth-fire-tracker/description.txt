A net worth tracker and FIRE calculator that shows everything you own and owe in one number, how close you are to financial independence, and the year you could get there. It opens in any browser and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Net Worth & FIRE Tracker is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add each account once: checking, savings, investments, retirement, your home, and what you owe on your mortgage, cards and loans. Once a month, a two-minute check-in updates each balance. Your net worth grows a point on the chart, you see what moved, and the FIRE plan works out your number, how far along you are, Coast FI, and the month you could stop working. No bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a fictional household's accounts first.
3. Add your accounts with today's balance. The usual ones (checking, savings, 401(k), IRA, brokerage, home, mortgage, credit card) are one click away.
4. Enter what you spend in a year. See your FIRE number, how far along you are, and when you could be financially independent.
5. Once a month, do the check-in: type each balance and watch your net worth and your progress move.
