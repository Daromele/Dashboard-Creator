A bill and subscription tracker that tells you what's due, what's paid and what renews next, opens in any browser, and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Bill & Subscription Tracker is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add each bill and subscription once: the amount, how often it comes and the next due date. From then on it lists what's due each month, lets you tick each one off as you pay it, warns you before free trials and yearly renewals charge, and adds up what it all costs a month and a year. No bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a fictional household's bills first.
3. Add your bills and subscriptions. Common ones (rent, electric, internet, phone, Netflix, Spotify, gym) are one click away.
4. Each month, tick bills off as you pay them. Bills on autopay tick themselves on their due day.
5. Check Renewals & trials now and then: cancel what you don't use, and see what it saves you.
