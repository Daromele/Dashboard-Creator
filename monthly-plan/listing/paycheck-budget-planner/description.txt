A paycheck to paycheck budget planner that puts every bill in the paycheck before it's due, gives your spending money an amount per paycheck, and shows what each paycheck leaves before you spend it. It opens in any browser and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Paycheck Budget Planner is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add your take-home pay, how often you're paid and your next payday, then your bills and your spending money for groceries, gas, fun and savings. From then on it works out which paycheck pays each bill, what each paycheck leaves after bills, spending and saving, and warns you when a paycheck coming up is going to run short, so you can move a bill to an earlier paycheck before it happens. Paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, with one income or two. No bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a fictional household's paychecks first.
3. Add your paycheck: take-home pay, how often, and your next payday. Add a partner's or side job's pay too.
4. Add your bills and your spending money. Common ones (rent, electric, phone, groceries, gas, savings) are one click away.
5. Each payday, open This paycheck: tick bills off as you pay them, log what you spend, and see what's left until the next one.
