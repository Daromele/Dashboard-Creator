A money tracker that reads your bank statements for you, opens in any browser, and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Income and Expense Tracker is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Drop in the CSV downloads from your bank and card accounts and it reads every line, cleans up the merchant names, sorts each one into a category and shows your month: money in, spending, what you kept, and where it all went. No typing, no bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a fictional household first.
3. Download the CSV from each bank and card (usually "Download" or "Export" on the transactions page) and drop them all in at once.
4. Check the few it isn't sure about in "Needs a look". It learns from every change you make, so next month needs even less.
5. Watch the dashboard. It shows where your money went, what repeats every month, and what you could keep.
