INCOME AND EXPENSE TRACKER | JPS DIGITAL PAGES

OPEN IT
Extract this ZIP, then open IncomeExpenseTracker.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on your computer. The HTML file is the app. Keep it somewhere easy to find, ideally a synced folder such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive. It works offline once open.

FIRST STEPS
A short welcome tour opens the first time. Then go to Add statements and drop in last month's CSV downloads from every bank and card, all at once. Clear the few in Needs a look, then read your Money picture.

SAMPLE
Explore sample data, on the welcome screen or in Settings & backup, fills every screen with a fictional household in a separate session. Return to my money restores your own figures.

STATEMENTS
On your bank or card website, open the transactions list and choose Download or Export, then CSV. PDF statements can't be read. Transactions already in the app are skipped, so overlapping files are safe. Card payments and your own transfers are paired and left out of spending.

BACK UP
Your data is saved in this browser on this device, not in the HTML file. Settings & backup > Download backup saves everything as one JSON file. Keep that file outside the app. To restore or move to another computer, copy the HTML file and your backup across and use Restore backup. Data does not sync automatically. Download a backup before clearing browser data or switching browsers.
Chrome and Edge can also write a dated backup to a folder you choose each day. Safari and Firefox do not support this; use Download backup instead.
