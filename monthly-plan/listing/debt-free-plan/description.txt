A debt payoff tracker that shows the exact month you'll be debt free, opens in any browser, and never asks you to sign up for anything.

Debt Free Plan is one HTML file. You download it, double-click it, and it opens like a web page. Add your debts once, choose how much you can pay each month, and it builds your full payoff plan: what to pay on each debt this month, when each one is gone, and how much interest you save. Log payments as you go and watch every balance fall. No spreadsheet formulas, no bank login, no subscription. Your figures stay on your own computer.

How It Works:
1. Download the file and save it somewhere you will find it again.
2. Double-click to open it in your browser. Take the two-minute tour, or open Sample Mode to explore a set of fictional debts first.
3. Add each debt once: balance, interest rate, minimum payment and due day. Or import your card and loan downloads.
4. Choose your monthly budget and a strategy. Avalanche, snowball and three more are compared side by side.
5. Log payments as you go, or import your statements, and watch your debt-free date move closer.
