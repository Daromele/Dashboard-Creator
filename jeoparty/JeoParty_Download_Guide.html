Your download guide: get the game open and playing in about two minutes.

🎃

1 What you've got

One file: JeoParty_-_Halloween_Edition_version_10.html. That single file is the whole game: ten built-in games, music, sound effects, themes and printables. There's nothing to install, no account and no subscription, and it works without internet.

2 Download it from Etsy

On a computer (recommended):

  1. Sign in at etsy.com and click your profile picture, then Purchases and reviews.
  2. Find JeoParty and click Download files.
  3. Click the file name to save it. It usually lands in your Downloads folder.
You can download it again anytime from Purchases and reviews, so there's no need to worry about losing it.

3 Move it somewhere permanent

Before you open it, move the file out of Downloads into a folder you'll keep, such as Documents › Party Games.

Why this matters: your teams, scores and My Games are saved by your browser for this file in this exact spot. If the file is moved, renamed or cleaned out of Downloads later, the game opens fresh and your saved games won't show up. Keep it in one place and always open it from there.

4 Open it

  1. Double-click the file. It opens in your web browser, just like a website.
  2. If it opens in a text editor or asks what to use, right-click the file and choose Open with › Google Chrome (or Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  3. You'll see the game board. Click anywhere once to wake up the sound.
Best in: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop. Safari and Firefox work too.

5 Your first game

  1. Click 🎃 Game Library in the top bar and pick the game that fits your guests, from Little Monsters (ages 5–10) to Horror Buff (film nerds only).
  2. Rename the teams in Settings, or keep the spooky defaults.
  3. Click Game Board, then click any tile to begin.
Tap the 🔈 speaker in the top bar to turn on spooky ambience and set the music and effects volume. The ❓ button opens the full in-game Help page.

📺 Play on the big screen

  • Connect your laptop to the TV with an HDMI cable, or cast the browser tab.
  • Press F11 (Windows) or Ctrl+⌘+F (Mac) for full screen.
  • Want to host from paper? Use the 🖨️ Print button for a Host Answer Key and Team Score Sheet.

📱 Phones & tablets

The game is designed for a computer. Phones often open HTML downloads in a preview that can't run games, so the board may look blank or frozen.

If that happens, open the file on a laptop or desktop instead.

⭐ My Games & backups

Edit any game's questions from Game Library › ✏️ Edit questions. Your first change saves a copy under My Games, and the original stays untouched.

Back up before the party. Go to Settings › Export Game Data to save a backup file with your games. To restore them on another computer or browser, use Settings › Import Game Data.

🛠️ Quick fixes

No soundClick anywhere on the page once; browsers block sound until you do. Check that Sound Effects is on in Settings and your device volume is up.
My saved games are goneOpen the file from the same folder, in the same browser, and not in a private/incognito window. Still missing? Use Import Game Data with your backup file.
The file shows codeIt opened in a text editor. Right-click it and choose Open with your web browser.
It looks slow on an older laptopIn Settings, turn Atmosphere down or off.
Start over completelySettings › Start Fresh erases everything, including My Games, so export a backup first.
