DESCRIPTION

A complete personal finance manager that lives in one file on your own computer. No subscription, no account, no bank connection - open it in your browser and your money is finally in one place.

You get ten connected screens: an overview with safe-to-spend, income, budget with rollover, transactions with CSV import, bills & subscriptions with a due-date calendar, savings goals with an emergency fund calculator, a debt payoff planner with snowball vs avalanche comparison, net worth over time, printable reports, and settings. Built for couples as much as singles: tag every item to a person or "joint" and see who contributes and who spends.

How It Works:
1. Download the ZIP and open Personal_Finance_Dashboard.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
2. Answer three setup questions (single or couple, currency, start month), or load the sample household to explore first.
3. Follow the welcome tour and the first-steps checklist: income, bills, accounts, budget. The calendar, cash-flow forecast and safe-to-spend figure build themselves from those.
4. Log spending as you go or import a CSV from your bank. Tick bills as paid. Watch the budget bars fill.
5. Turn on automatic backup in Settings (Chrome/Edge) or export a JSON backup any time.
